Questions

Questions about homes & apartments

Straight answers. If yours is not here, ask us.

Ask us something else
Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. Many clients leave a key or arrange access with the caretaker, and we agree how access works when you book. For a first clean we suggest being there for the walk-through at the end.

Do you do Airbnb turnovers between guests?

Yes. Linen changed on request, bathrooms and kitchen reset, the unit made ready for the next check-in — scheduled around your bookings.

Can you clean sofas on site?

Yes. Sofa sets, chairs and upholstery are cleaned in place: stains pre-treated, fabric shampooed, cushions and crevices done. Drying time depends on the fabric, and we tell you before we start.

How long does a move-out clean take?

It depends on the size of the home and the state it is in, so we confirm the time when we scope the job. Tell us your handover date and we plan to it.
