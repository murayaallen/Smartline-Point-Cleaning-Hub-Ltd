# Cleaning sector — what good websites do, and what we took

Research done 17/11/2026 for `smartlinepointcleaning.co.ke`. Sites studied in three
groups: premium commercial cleaners (UK), a global facility-services leader, and the
Nairobi competitors Smartline is actually measured against. Everything marked
**Taken** is already in `site_layout.py` and visible in `wireframes.html`.

---

## 1. What the premium end does

**[Cleanology](https://cleanology.com)** (London, ~1,400 staff) and
**[Julius Rutherfoord & Co](https://www.juliusrutherfoord.co.uk)** (London, 1,200 staff,
B Corp) are the two best-presented commercial cleaners found. Both sell on the same
thing, and it is not cleaning.

| What they do | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sell the **workforce**, not the mop | JR&Co's line is *"The power of undivided attention"*. Cleanology's "DNA" is People / Planet / Future. The service is assumed; the people are the argument. |
| Put **vetting in the main navigation** | Cleanology has *Our People → Management, Training, Vetting & Security* and a separate *Quality Control* page. Nobody buys a cleaning contract without asking who is coming into the building. |
| Organise by **sector**, not by task | JR&Co sells Offices · Science & Tech · Education · Heritage. Cleanology runs ~15 sector pages. A facility manager looks for their own building type. |
| Lead with **hard numbers** | 25+ years · 500+ reviews · 1,400 staff · 94% retention · 34 nationalities (Cleanology); 1994 · 1,200 staff · 200 London clients (JR&Co). |
| **Name clients** | Freshfields, Knight Frank, JLL, the Supreme Court, the Italian Embassy, UNIQLO. Logos, not adjectives. |
| **No prices anywhere** | Both quote only. At enterprise level, publishing a rate card loses more than it wins. |
| Keep a **recruitment door open** | Cleanology puts *Apply for a job* in the header; ISS has a whole *Grow with us* section. Cleaning firms hire constantly. |

**[ISS](https://www.issworld.com)** (125 years, 330,000 staff, 40,000 customers) does the
same at enterprise scale: outcome-led copy ("We make space for people and businesses to
thrive"), services × industries as a matrix, CTAs that are *Talk to our experts* and
*Let's work together* rather than *Buy*.

**Taken:**
- **Vetting, insurance and compliance** is now its own section on About (A-06) — staff
  vetting, direct employment, NSSF/SHIF, insurance, approved chemicals, waste. None of
  the Nairobi sites answer this at all; it is the cheapest competitive gap on the list.
- **Sector chips** on the services page (S-08) — offices, banks, schools, churches,
  apartment blocks, Airbnb, hospitality, new builds — linking to the clients page. The
  cheap version of sector pages, no extra pages used.
- **Work with us** block on About (A-08), applications to operations@. It also makes the
  job-creation line in their vision visible instead of merely stated.
- Numbers in the proof strip and client names high on the home page: already in the plan,
  and confirmed as the pattern the best sites follow.

## 2. What the Nairobi market does

| | [Bestcare](https://bestcareservices.co.ke/professional-commercial-cleaning-in-nairobi-and-kenya/) | [Nairobi Cleaning Service Centre](https://nairobicleaningservice.co.ke) |
|---|---|---|
| Prices | Full KES table: basic office **50–80/m²**, deep disinfection 100–150/m², floor polishing 80–120/m², windows 200–400/pane, carpet 5,000–15,000/room | Monthly tiers: **15,000 / 35,000 / 60,000+**, carpet 300/m², deep clean from 2,500 |
| Guarantee | "Satisfaction guarantee", 98% retention claim | **Re-clean free if reported within 24 hours** |
| Contact | Click-to-call everywhere, WhatsApp, quote page | Two numbers, WhatsApp, form with service dropdown, M-Pesa accepted |
| Trust | NEMA / KRA / NCA / KEBS references, a case study ("Westlands law firm, 30% fewer sick days") | 20+ years, 500+ businesses, 4.9★, insured and vetted staff |
| Weaknesses | Thin duplicated SEO pages, unverifiable claims ("99.9% pathogen elimination") with no source | Empty blog ("No posts found"), one unlabelled per-m² price, repeated identical CTAs |

Published rates disagree wildly between sources — KES 15–80/m² for the same described
work — so treat all of it as positioning, not a market rate.
([achisystems](https://achisystems.co.ke/?p=27507),
[hudumaglobal](https://hudumaglobal.com/blog/commercial-cleaning-services-kenya-diaspora))

**The pricing decision, with evidence.** Both large Nairobi competitors publish numbers.
The London firms publish none. Smartline sits between: it sells contracts to facility
managers *and* one-off cleans to households. Showing nothing makes it the least
transparent of the three sites a Nairobi buyer will compare.

**Taken:** a **guide-price block** on the services page (S-07) — three tiers (per clean,
monthly contract, per m²) with *"the final figure comes after a site visit"*. It answers
the first question every visitor has without surrendering the site visit, which is where
Smartline actually wins the job. It needs three figures from the client, or a decision to
show none; the section is flagged for that.

**Taken:** the **24-hour re-clean promise** is now a band of its own on the home page
(H-09), with the seal, the promise in one sentence, and the three things behind it
(named supervisor, checked against the written scope, monthly review). The competitor
publishes the same promise — this is parity, not generosity, and it is the single
sentence most likely to turn a visitor into an enquiry.

## 3. Conversion patterns

Consistent across every source, though all of them are vendor blogs and none publishes a
trustworthy benchmark rate
([ContractorPlus](https://contractorplus.app/blog/how-one-cleaning-company-doubled-bookings-with-a-better-online-request-form),
[PowerWash](https://powerwash.com/spray-tips/tips-to-generate-more-leads-from-your-pressure-washing-business-website/),
[CleaningBusinessGrowth](https://cleaningbusinessgrowth.com/?p=27051)):

- Short forms beat long ones; one case cut a four-page form to seven questions. **Our
  two-step form with six required fields already matches this.**
- Ask the job first, the person second — lower commitment to start.
- Put **proof next to the form**. **Taken:** the contact page (K-02) now carries a
  testimonial, the guarantee and the vetting line directly under the submit button.
- Specific CTA wording beats "Contact us". Our CTAs are *Request a quote*, *Book your
  clean today*, *Get a quote for office cleaning*.
- Reply speed decides who wins the job. **Taken:** *"Reply within one working day"* is now
  a trust chip in the hero, not just a line on the contact page. The thank-you page already
  sets the same expectation.
- **[Justlife](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/cleaning-app-dubai/)** (Dubai, formerly
  Justmop) publishes an hourly rate and books in under a minute. Full instant booking is
  out of scope here, but it is the direction the guide-price block can grow into once the
  rate card is fixed — logged in the roadmap.

## 4. Local search

Google's own three factors are relevance, distance and prominence; practitioner surveys
put the **primary category** as the single heaviest lever and reviews at roughly a fifth
of local ranking weight
([Whitespark/BrightLocal summaries](https://seahawkmedia.com/wordpress/local-seo-ranking-factors-guide/),
[Loganix](https://loganix.com/google-local-search-ranking-factors/)).

- Primary category **Commercial cleaning service**; secondaries for house cleaning,
  carpet cleaning and landscaping.
- Set up as a **service-area business** covering Nairobi and Kiambu.
- One blanket "areas we serve" page rarely ranks — a page per area does. **Logged as
  phase 2** (Kasarani, Ruiru, Roysambu, Westlands, CBD), outside the quoted eight pages.
- Reviews are both a ranking and a conversion lever: ask on completion, with a QR card.
- Google strips stock photography from profiles — this is why `REAL-07` (ten real photos)
  is on the client list and cannot be filled with anything we download.

## 5. What we deliberately did not copy

- **Unverifiable claims.** "99.9% pathogen elimination", invented retention percentages,
  uncited surveys. Everything on Smartline's site must be true and checkable; the
  vetting table is flagged *publish nothing we cannot evidence*.
- **An empty blog.** A competitor ships "No posts found". We launch without a blog and add
  one only when there is something to say.
- **A wall of identical CTAs.** Flagged as a weakness on the Nairobi sites; our CTA
  wording changes with context.
- **Split funnels.** [AspenClean](https://www.aspenclean.com) runs a product shop and a
  cleaning service from one home page and the attention splits. One funnel: the quote.
- **Price-gated booking with no numbers at all.** [Alpine Maids](https://alpinemaids.com)
  hides everything behind *Book Now*; in a market where two competitors publish tables,
  that reads as evasive.

## 6. Net effect on the plan

Home 10 → 11 sections, About 8 → 10, Services 8 → 10. Four new sections, one changed
hero chip, one new block beside the contact form, six items on a phase-2 roadmap
(`LAYOUT.md` §7a). Still eight pages, still inside the quoted build.

Needs a decision from the client: **three guide-price figures, or none** (S-07), and
**confirmation of which compliance lines are true today, with documents** (A-06).
