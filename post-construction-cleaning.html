Post-construction cleaning in Nairobi

From builders' dust to handover-ready — phased, to your handover date.

Request a quote WhatsApp us

Overview

Who we do this for

ContractorsDevelopersNew homeownersRenovated officesProperty managers

Builders finish; the dust does not. Fine dust settles for days, paint and cement splatter set hard, and glass, frames and tracks hold everything the trades left behind. Post-construction cleaning is its own discipline.

Smartline phases the work to your handover date: a rough clean once the trades are out, a final clean before handover and a touch-up after snagging. Contractors, developers and owners get a space ready for people, not just finished.

Questions

Questions about post-construction

Straight answers. If yours is not here, ask us.

Ask us something else
When in the build should we book?

As soon as you know your handover date. The clean is phased — a rough clean once the trades finish, a final clean before handover, a touch-up after snagging — so the earlier we see the site, the better the plan.

Do you remove construction debris?

Yes. Debris from the clean is cleared, bagged and removed from site. For large volumes of rubble we agree the arrangement when we scope the job.

Can you work to a handover date?

Yes. We work back from your date and phase the rough clean, final clean and touch-up so the space is ready when the keys change hands.

Do you clean occupied buildings after renovation?

Yes. Offices and homes that stay in use during works get the same post-construction clean, scheduled around the people using them.
