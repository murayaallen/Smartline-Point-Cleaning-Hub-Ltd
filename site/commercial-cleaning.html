Overview

Who we do this for

OfficesBanksSchoolsChurchesMalls and complexesLounges and hospitality

A clean office is noticed only when it is not. Smartline runs scheduled cleaning for offices, banks, schools, churches and commercial complexes across Nairobi — daily, weekly or on a programme — with a trained team and a named supervisor who knows your premises.

We learn how your building runs before we clean it: when people arrive, which areas carry the most traffic, what your staff and visitors expect. The scope is agreed in writing, checked on every visit and reviewed monthly, so the standard holds long after the first week.

Questions

Questions about commercial cleaning

Straight answers. If yours is not here, ask us.

Ask us something else
Can you clean outside office hours?

Yes. Most offices are cleaned early in the morning or after close so your people never work around a mop. Banks, schools and churches get a schedule built around their own hours.

Do you supply equipment and chemicals?

Yes — machines, tools and products suited to your floors and surfaces, handled by cleaners trained in correct dilution and storage. Consumables such as soap and tissue can be supplied or restocked from yours.

Can the same cleaners stay on our site?

Yes, and that is the point. Your site keeps the same team wherever possible, under one named supervisor, so the people cleaning learn how your premises run.

What happens if a cleaner is absent?

The supervisor arranges cover from the trained team so the agreed scope is still delivered. Cover is our problem to solve, not yours to chase.
