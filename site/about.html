About Smartline

A professional cleaning company in Nairobi — one point of contact for every clean, since 2018.

Our story

Founded to take cleaning off your list

Smartline was established in 2018 to let businesses focus on their core work while hygiene and cleaning are handled through a single point of contact. Our staff are trained all round, well groomed and experienced enough to take on any cleaning task.

We learn each client's culture and ethics and align our cleaners' training to your standards. We support and equip our teams to make a difference you can see, and we customise the programme — comprehensive, quarterly, semi-annual or annual — to how your premises actually run.

Purpose

Vision and mission

Vision

To become a leading cleaning services provider while contributing to the community through job creation and corporate social responsibility.

Mission

Simplifying the lives of our clients — so they concentrate on the work that earns, not on cleaners and facility problems.

Our approach

We learn your business before we clean it.

We take time to understand each client's culture and ethics, and align our cleaners' training to your standards. We support and equip our teams to make a visible difference, and we customise the programme — comprehensive, quarterly, semi-annual or annual — to how your premises actually run.

Training

How our cleaners are trained

Six areas, covered before anyone is placed on a site — and refreshed by the supervisor on the job.

Proper communication

Clear, courteous with clients, staff and residents.

Equipment use

Machines, mops and tools used correctly and safely.

Chemical handling

Right product, right dilution, stored and labelled.

Grooming and PPE

Uniformed, presentable, protected on every job.

Teamwork

Supervised teams that cover for each other.

Code of conduct

Honesty, confidentiality and respect for your premises.
