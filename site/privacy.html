Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal data under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

Smartline Point Cleaning Hub Ltd (“Smartline”, “we”), ICIPE Road, Sports Drive, Suite 8, Kasarani, Nairobi, P.O. Box 48018-00100, Nairobi. We decide how the personal data described here is used, under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

When you request a quote or contact us: your name, phone number, email address if you give it, your organisation if you give it, the location of the premises and the details of the job. When you call, email or WhatsApp us, the same details you choose to share.

Why we use it

To reply to you, arrange a site visit, prepare a quote and deliver the service you ask for. We rely on your consent, given on the form, and on taking the steps you have asked for before a contract.

Cookies

This site uses no advertising cookies. If analytics is switched on, it runs only after you accept it in the cookie notice, and you can change your mind at any time from the link in the footer.

Who we share it with

No one, except as needed to run this website and our email. We do not sell or rent personal data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as the contract runs and any period the law requires after it.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. Write to info@smartlinepointcleaning.co.ke with “Data request” in the subject. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
