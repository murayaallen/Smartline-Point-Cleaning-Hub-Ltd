Questions

Questions we are asked

The things clients ask before they book. Anything else, call or WhatsApp.

Ask us something else
Do you bring your own equipment and chemicals?

Yes. Our teams arrive equipped with the machines, tools and cleaning products the job needs, and every cleaner is trained in correct dilution and safe handling. If your premises have products you prefer, tell us and we will work with them.

Do you charge for a site visit?

For contract work and larger jobs we see the space before we price it, so the quote matches the real premises. For smaller one-off cleans we can often quote from a few photographs sent on WhatsApp. We tell you which applies on the first call, before any cost is involved.

Can you clean outside working hours?

Yes. Early mornings, evenings and weekends are routine for offices, schools and churches. Cleaning should fit around your work, not interrupt it.

Are your cleaners vetted?

Every cleaner is trained before placement — communication, equipment use, chemical handling, grooming and PPE, teamwork and our code of conduct — and works under a named supervisor who is accountable for your site.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Kasarani and work across Nairobi and Kiambu, including Roysambu, Ruiru, Thika Road, the CBD and Westlands. Outside those areas, ask: for contract work we often can.
